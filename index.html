Whole-body control on real humanoids.

I am a graduate research assistant in the Correll Lab at CU Boulder, working on contact-rich whole-body control for humanoid robots. My current work runs sampling-based whole-body MPC on a Unitree H1-2 with only local sensing and fiducial markers, no motion capture, and has the robot brace against a table to reach objects far outside its standing workspace.

Before humanoids I worked on constrained motion planning for robotic EV battery disassembly, CubeSat attitude control, and a string of things that fly: a ground-effect aircraft, autonomous quadcopters, a CanSat, and a sounding rocket.

M.S. Aerospace Engineering Sciences, CU Boulder, 2027. B.E. Aeronautical Engineering, MIT Anna University, 2025.

Supported reaching with whole-body MPC on the Unitree H1-2

Correll Lab, CU BoulderJan 2026 – present

A sampling-based whole-body MPC framework, built on MuJoCo MPC, for leaning, bracing, reaching, and recovery on a real Unitree H1-2. The robot braces one forearm on a table to extend its reachable workspace by about 50 cm beyond unbraced standing, holds the loaded posture for over four minutes, and returns to standing. The controller replans at 34 Hz on a laptop and transfers sim-to-real.

  • Local sensing only: a proprioceptive estimator fused with AprilTag fiducials from the head camera, no motion capture
  • Reaches the commanded target in 89% of trials and completes the full reach-and-recovery cycle in 81%, over 54 hardware trials spanning six targets and three table heights, with end-effector accuracy of 2.7 ± 0.9 cm
  • Planner study: temporal persistence of sampled actions and exploration scale govern success at small sampling budgets, and CEM gives the widest operating region over planning rate and model mismatch

Robotic disassembly of EV battery packs

Correll Lab, CU BoulderJan 2026 – present

A constrained motion planning pipeline for a 7-DOF industrial manipulator on a gantry: analytical IK seed ranking and cost-normalised configuration selection to reach every screw on a full-size pack. A vision-language model reasons over the pointcloud about which screws remain, closing the loop between perception and the planner.

  • Co-author on RAPID, IEEE CASE 2026, and on the GOLEM humanoid autonomy stack
  • Presented the project to industry and government stakeholders at the ARPA-E Energy Innovation Summit, April 2026
